Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains how the Social Media Agent desktop application (the “Service”) handles information when you use its platform integrations. The Service is in development and is designed to run locally on your computer.

1. Information handled

  • Account identifiers and profile or statistics fields you authorize from a connected platform.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to keep an authorized connection.
  • Videos, captions, and other content you select for processing or upload.
  • Basic technical information returned by platform APIs, such as upload status.

2. How information is used

The Service uses this information to connect the accounts you choose, read the platform data allowed by your permissions, and perform actions you initiate. For TikTok draft uploads, the selected video and caption are sent to TikTok so TikTok can create a draft for your account. You review, edit, and publish the draft in TikTok.

3. Local storage and sharing

The development version stores OAuth credentials and tokens in a local .env file on your computer. The project operator does not receive those values through a hosted application server. Keep that file private and delete it from your device when you no longer need the connection. Requests and content are sent to the platform whose integration you use (for example, TikTok or Google/YouTube) to provide the requested feature.

If you choose to use an optional AI provider integration, prompts and content you submit for that feature may be sent to the provider configured by you, such as OpenAI, and are also subject to that provider's privacy terms.

4. Retention and control

Local credentials remain on your device until you remove them. You can revoke the Service's authorization in the connected platform's account settings. Drafts or posts already uploaded are held by the platform and can be managed there.

5. Website hosting

This information page is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information under its own privacy policy when you visit this site.

6. Security and contact

Protect your computer and do not share your .env file. To ask a privacy question or request help, contact diasruc@gmail.com.
