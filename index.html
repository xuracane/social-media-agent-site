Social Media Agent

A desktop tool in development for planning and preparing social media content. For TikTok, users can connect their account and upload selected videos as drafts, then review, edit, and publish them in TikTok.

How it works

You choose the content and authorize the integrations you want to use. The tool requests only the platform permissions needed for those features. TikTok draft uploads are initiated by you; final editing and publishing take place in TikTok.

Availability of platform integrations depends on each platform's approval and API access requirements.
